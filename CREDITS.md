# Third-Party Asset Credits

## Hero animation base template
- **Source:** "Isometric Character Template" by intellikat (https://intellikat.itch.io), with thanks to sgeos for support
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain) — attribution not legally required but explicitly requested by the author
- **Used for:** `godot/assets/sprites/heroes/<variant_id>/*.png` — the front/rear run, stand, pistol, and sleep cycles were recolored per hero (skin/clothing tones derived from each hero's portrait) and reassembled into the horizontal sprite strips `AnimationLoader.gd` expects. Left/right walk frames are a mirrored reuse of the front cycle — the source template only authors front (toward-camera) and rear (away-from-camera) views.

## Hero portrait art
- Portrait images at `godot/assets/heroes/portraits/*.png` were supplied directly by the project owner.
