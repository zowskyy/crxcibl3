# Privacy Policy — CRXCIBL3

**App name:** CRXCIBL3  
**Package name:** `com.zowskyy.crxcibl3`  
**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

This privacy policy describes how CRXCIBL3 ("the app," "we," "our") handles information when you install and play the game on your device.

---

## Summary

CRXCIBL3 does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The app works fully offline and stores game progress only on your device.

---

## Information we do not collect

We do **not** collect:

- Name, email address, phone number, or other contact information
- Location data (GPS or network-based)
- Device identifiers for tracking or advertising
- Usage analytics or crash telemetry
- Payment or purchase information (the app has no in-app purchases)
- Photos, contacts, microphone, or camera data

There is no account registration and no login.

---

## Local data on your device

The app saves game progress to a file in its private application storage:

- **File:** `crxcibl3_save.txt`
- **Path (Godot):** `user://crxcibl3_save.txt`
- **Contents:** Game state only — for example heat level, squad selection, act progress, relationship values, boss outcomes, and similar gameplay flags.

This file:

- Stays on your device
- Is not uploaded to our servers (we operate no backend for this app)
- Is not accessible to other apps under normal Android sandbox rules
- Is removed if you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app

You may delete your save at any time by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

---

## Network access

CRXCIBL3 is designed to run **offline**. The app does not require an internet connection to play and does not send data over the network as part of normal gameplay.

---

## Third-party services and SDKs

CRXCIBL3 does **not** integrate third-party analytics, advertising, social login, or crash-reporting SDKs. Gameplay does not embed external tracking libraries.

The app is built with the [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org/) (open-source). Standard Android system libraries required to run the app may apply platform-level policies governed by Google; CRXCIBL3 itself does not add separate data-collection SDKs.

---

## Children's privacy

CRXCIBL3 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone because the app does not collect personal information at all. The game contains mature themes and is intended for older audiences per store content ratings.

---

## Data security

Because data never leaves your device, there is no remote database or cloud sync to secure. Save files reside in the app's sandboxed storage provided by Android.

---

## Your rights

Depending on your region, privacy laws may grant rights regarding personal data. CRXCIBL3 does not process personal data; there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on our systems. You control local save data through your device (clear app data or uninstall).
